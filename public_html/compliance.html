Regulatory Landscape

Pakistan's Healthcare Regulatory Framework

🏛️

National Level

  • PM&DC - Pakistan Medical & Dental Council
  • DRAP - Drug Regulatory Authority of Pakistan
  • EPA - Environmental Protection Agency
  • PHC - Provincial Health Council
  • PNRA - Pakistan Nuclear Regulatory Authority
  • PN&MC - Pakistan Nursing & Midwifery Council
  • AHPC - Allied Health Professionals Council
🌍

Provincial Level

  • PHC - Punjab Healthcare Commission
  • KPHCC - Khyber Pakhtunkhwa Healthcare Commission
  • IHRA - Islamabad Healthcare Regulatory Authority
  • SHCC - Sindh Healthcare Commission
  • BHCC - Baluchistan Healthcare Commission
🌐

International Standards

  • JCIA - Japan Council for International Healthcare
What We Offer

Our Compliance Services

🏛️ Compliance Assessment

The Healthcare Commissions/Regulatory Authority sets critical standards for healthcare facility operations. We help you:

  • Conduct comprehensive regulatory compliance audits
  • Develop facility policies aligned with regulatory standards
  • Implement required documentation systems
  • Prepare for regulatory registration and renewal
  • Maintain ongoing regulatory compliance

📋 MSDS Implementation

Minimum Service Delivery Standards (MSDS) define quality benchmarks for healthcare delivery. We assist in:

  • MSDS compliance gap analysis
  • Development of MSDS compliance roadmap
  • Staff training on MSDS requirements
  • Implementation of quality management systems
  • Ongoing MSDS compliance monitoring

⚕️ PM&DC Registration & Compliance

Pakistan Medical & Dental Council (PM&DC) compliance ensures professional standards:

  • Coordination with PM&DC for facility registration
  • Professional standards compliance
  • Staff qualification verification
  • Continuing education requirements management
  • Professional conduct policy development

🏥 Provincial Commission Compliance

Each province has specific health care commission requirements:

  • PHC - Punjab Healthcare Commission
  • IHRA - Islamabad Healthcare Regulatory Authority
  • KPHCC - Khyber Pakhtunkhwa compliance
  • SHCC - Sindh Health Care Commission requirements
  • BHCC - Balochistan Health Care Commission standards
  • Province-specific documentation and reporting
  • Regular compliance reporting and renewal

📂 Documentation & Record Management

Comprehensive regulatory documentation systems:

  • Compliance documentation development
  • Standard operating procedures (SOPs) creation
  • Record management system implementation
  • Digital documentation solutions
  • Audit trail and compliance tracking

🔍 Regulatory Audit Preparation

With 3,000+ audits conducted, we ensure you're audit-ready:

  • Pre-audit facility walk-through
  • Identification of compliance gaps
  • Documentation preparation and organization
  • Staff briefing and training
  • Post-audit support and remediation
