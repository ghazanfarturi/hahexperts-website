What We Offer

Expert Healthcare Solutions

📋 Regulatory Compliance

Navigate Pakistan's complex healthcare regulatory landscape with confidence. Our services ensure your facility meets all national and provincial requirements:

  • IHRA Compliance: Institute of Health Regulatory Authority requirements and standards
  • MSDS Implementation: Minimum Service Delivery Standards framework development
  • PM&DC Standards: Pakistan Medical & Dental Council registration and compliance
  • Provincial Commission: KPHCC, SHCC, BHCC requirements specific to your region
  • Regulatory Audits: Comprehensive compliance assessments and audit preparation
  • Documentation: Complete compliance documentation and record management

🔬 Biomedical Engineering

From equipment selection to BSL-3 laboratory commissioning, we provide comprehensive biomedical engineering support:

  • Equipment Lifecycle: Selection, procurement, installation, maintenance, decommissioning
  • Medical Procurement: Vendor evaluation, specification development, quality assurance
  • Safety Assessments: Comprehensive safety audits and risk mitigation
  • BSL-3 Labs: Biosafety level 3 laboratory design and operational protocols
  • Equipment Training: Staff training and competency development
  • Technical Compliance: ISO and international biomedical standards

⚖️ Legal Advisory

Navigate the complex legal landscape of healthcare with our specialized advisory services:

  • Healthcare Law: Guidance on healthcare regulations and legal requirements
  • Compliance Documentation: Legal compliance policies and procedures
  • Risk Management: Identification and mitigation of legal and operational risks
  • Legal Representation: Support in complaints before different Forums i.e., Courts, Regulatory Bodies etc.
  • Regulatory Assistance: Support in discussions with regulatory bodies
  • Policy Development: Facility-specific policies aligned with regulations
  • Legal Drafting: Will assist in preparing of Contracts, MoU etc

🛒 Procurement Services

Optimize your procurement processes with our specialized sourcing and vendor management:

  • Equipment Sourcing: Identification and procurement of quality equipment
  • Vendor Evaluation: Comprehensive vendor assessment and selection
  • Quality Assurance: Product quality verification and compliance checks
  • Tender Management: RFQ development and tender process management
  • Cost Optimization: Negotiation strategies for best value procurement
  • Supply Chain: Logistics and delivery coordination

🏥 Healthcare Management

Transform your healthcare facility's operations and clinical governance:

  • Clinical Governance: Comprehensive governance structure development
  • EMR Implementation: Electronic Medical Record system selection and deployment
  • Quality Management: ISO-aligned quality management program development
  • Operational Efficiency: Process optimization and resource utilization
  • Staff Training: Comprehensive training programs for healthcare personnel
  • Anti-Quackery: Measures against unqualified practitioners
