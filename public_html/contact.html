Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

⏱️ How long does a compliance audit take?

The duration depends on your facility's size and complexity. A typical compliance audit takes 3-7 days for medium-sized facilities. We'll provide a detailed timeline during our initial consultation.

💰 What is the cost of your services?

Our services are customized based on your facility's specific needs. We provide detailed quotes after understanding your requirements. Contact us for a free initial consultation.

🔧 Can you help with existing compliance issues?

Yes. Our team specializes in identifying and remediating existing compliance gaps. We'll work with you to develop corrective action plans and ensure regulatory readiness.

🏥 Do you serve small clinics?

Absolutely. We serve healthcare facilities of all sizes, from small clinics to large tertiary care institutions. We have flexible service packages tailored to your needs.

📅 How often should we get audited?

At minimum, annual compliance audits are recommended. We suggest quarterly check-ins for facilities in critical transition periods or those addressing specific compliance issues.

📋 What's your experience with MSDS standards?

Our CEO has extensive expertise in MSDS (Minimum Service Delivery Standards) implementation with a track record of 3,000+ audits. We're specialists in MSDS compliance across all service delivery areas.
